### Q2: 讲一个你带团队解决困难问题的例子。/ Tell me about a time you led your team through a tough problem.

**中文版：**
> 去年我们给空天院客户部署 JetIce 平台，部署完成后出现了严重的网络丢包问题，直接影响业务。
>
> 我组织团队做了系统性排查：先从网卡驱动入手（排除固件问题），然后查内核参数（rp_filter、netdev_budget），再看 OVS 和 DPDK 的配置。最终定位到是 DPDK 的 CPU 绑定策略和内核参数的组合问题。
>
> 整个过程我负责指挥排查方向，把复杂问题拆解成独立可验证的假设，安排团队成员分别验证。这次排查完以后，我们输出了一份标准的 RCA 报告和一份"网络排查 SOP"，后续类似问题处理时间从 4 小时缩短到了 30 分钟。

**English Version：**
> After deploying JetIce at an aerospace institute customer site, we hit severe network packet loss that was impacting their operations immediately after cutover.
>
> I led the team through a structured RCA process: we started with NIC driver diagnostics, moved to kernel parameter tuning (rp_filter, netdev_budget), then investigated OVS and DPDK configuration. We eventually isolated the root cause to a conflict between DPDK CPU affinity settings and kernel parameters.
>
> My role was to decompose the problem into independently verifiable hypotheses and assign each one to a team member in parallel. After resolution, I wrote a formal RCA report and a network troubleshooting SOP. That SOP reduced our response time for similar issues from 4 hours to under 30 minutes.

## 三、可观测性技术问题

### Q3: 你是如何设计监控体系的？/ How do you design an observability stack?

**中文版：**
> 我的可观测性设计遵循三层模型：**指标（Metrics）→ 日志（Logs）→ 链路追踪（Traces）**。
>
> 在实际项目中，我搭建的体系是：
> - **指标层**：Prometheus 采集，Grafana 可视化，针对不同资源类型（计算/存储/网络）分别建仪表盘
> - **日志层**：Fluentd/FluentBit 采集 → Loki 存储 → Grafana 展示，支持结构化查询
> - **告警层**：Prometheus AlertManager + Grafana Alert，分级告警（P1 立即通知，P2 工单，P3 日报）
>
> 针对数据平台，我会在这个基础上加：Spark job 执行状态（成功率/延迟/重试次数）、Kafka consumer lag、数据管道 SLA 达成率这些业务级指标，而不是只看基础设施层的 CPU/内存。

**English Version：**
> I design observability stacks around the three pillars: **Metrics, Logs, and Traces**.
>
> In practice, the stack I've built includes:
> - **Metrics**: Prometheus scraping, Grafana dashboards segmented by resource type (compute, storage, network)
> - **Logs**: Fluentd/FluentBit → Loki → Grafana, with structured log querying
> - **Alerting**: Prometheus AlertManager with tiered severity — P1 for immediate page, P2 for ticket, P3 for daily digest
>
> For a data platform specifically, I'd extend this with business-level metrics: Spark job success rate and latency, Kafka consumer lag, and data pipeline SLA compliance — not just infrastructure-level CPU/memory. That's what makes SRE meaningful to the business.

### Q4: 你如何处理告警疲劳？/ How do you handle alert fatigue?

**中文版：**
> 告警疲劳通常来自三个问题：**阈值不合理、告警不分级、告警没人认领**。
>
> 我的做法：
> 1. **每条告警必须对应一个 runbook**：没有处理步骤的告警不应该存在
> 2. **分级机制**：P1 是"必须立刻处理"，P2 是"今天内处理"，P3 是"下周 sprint 评审"
> 3. **定期告警审查**：每两周看一次"这两周触发了哪些告警，哪些是噪音"，持续调优阈值
> 4. **告警去重和静默**：维护窗口期间自动静默相关告警

**English Version：**
> Alert fatigue comes from three root causes: **bad thresholds, no severity tiers, and unclear ownership**.
>
> My approach:
> 1. **Every alert must have a runbook** — no action, no alert. Alerts without clear response steps get deleted.
> 2. **Severity tiering**: P1 means "wake someone up now", P2 is "handle today", P3 is "review in sprint"
> 3. **Bi-weekly alert review**: Look at what fired over the past two weeks, identify noise, and continuously refine thresholds
> 4. **Alert silencing during maintenance windows** — automated suppression to prevent noise during planned changes

## 四、自动化与 SRE 实践

### Q5: 讲一个你通过自动化减少手工操作的例子。/ Give an example of automation reducing toil.

**中文版：**
> 最典型的例子是我们的物理机装机（BMAAS）流程。
>
> 原来的流程：运维工程师需要手动准备 ISO、配置 BIOS、一台台装机，一个站点部署大概需要 2–3 天。
>
> 我重构了整个流程：输入只需要服务器的 MAC 地址，系统自动：识别硬件型号 → 选择对应 OS 镜像 → 通过 Tinkerbell 触发 PXE 装机 → 自动配置多网卡 Bonding → cloud-init 初始化 → 冒烟测试验证。
>
> 结果：一个新客户站点（扬州电信）的完整装机时间从 3 天缩短到 4 小时，工程师全程只需要确认最终状态。这就是 toil reduction 的核心——把重复的、可预测的操作变成代码。

**English Version：**
> A strong example is our bare-metal provisioning (BMaaS) transformation.
>
> Before: Engineers manually prepared ISOs, configured BIOS settings, and installed servers one by one — a new site deployment took 2–3 days.
>
> After my redesign: The only input is a list of MAC addresses. The system automatically identifies hardware, selects the appropriate OS image, triggers PXE boot via Tinkerbell, configures multi-NIC bonding, runs cloud-init initialization, and validates with smoke tests.
>
> Result: A full new customer site deployment (Yangzhou Telecom) went from 3 days to 4 hours. Engineers only confirm the final state. That's the core of toil elimination — turning predictable, repetitive operations into code.

### Q6: 你对 SLI/SLO/SLA 是怎么理解和实践的？/ How do you define and use SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs?

**中文版：**
> SLI 是你实际测量什么，SLO 是你的目标，SLA 是你对外承诺什么。
>
> 实践中，我习惯从业务出发定义 SLI，而不是从基础设施出发。比如，对于一个数据管道服务，SLI 不是"机器 CPU 低于 80%"，而是"数据从产生到可查询的延迟"。
>
> 在 IBM，我们为工具链服务定义了 4 小时内恢复（RTO）、零数据丢失（RPO = 0）的 SLO，并通过变更管理流程来保护这个目标。每次 incident 复盘都会检查是否违反了 SLO，并更新错误预算。

**English Version：**
> SLIs are what you measure, SLOs are your targets, SLAs are your external commitments.
>
> In practice, I always define SLIs from the business perspective, not from infrastructure. For a data pipeline, the right SLI is "latency from data generation to queryable availability," not "server CPU utilization below 80%."
>
> At IBM, we defined SLOs of 4-hour RTO and zero data loss (RPO=0) for our toolchain services, with error budgets managed through change control. Every incident postmortem included a review of whether we breached an SLO, and we used that data to update our reliability roadmap.
